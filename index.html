✨ Revolutionizing Wedding Management Digitally

sagun Management System (SMS) is a state-of-the-art digital wedding platform conceptualized and developed to eliminate the hassles and discrepancies of traditional paper-based record-keeping during marriages and family events. From managing guest arrivals seamlessly to recording every single cash, gift, or UPI shagun entry accurately, our system brings 100% transparency, speed, and absolute security to your celebrations.
